Mappings

A mapping says which column of a coverholder's spreadsheet is the gross premium. Every one of them has a person's name on it: confirmed_by is not nullable in the schema, so a mapping that a model proposed and nobody accepted cannot be in this table. Mappings are versioned rather than edited, because re-deriving a period under a new version must leave last month's numbers attributable to the version that produced them.
