The scored hold-out

Six of eighteen schema variants, chosen by blake2b(variant_id) and by the unseen-vocabulary flag — a rule that consults no seed and no score, so it cannot be re-drawn to flatter a result. ADR-002 records what was observed before the scoring run and what changed in between.

Mapping, against the four predeclared baselines
